About Me

The Art of Seeing

Hello, I'm Alex Rivera — a passionate photographer with over a decade of experience capturing the beauty of life through my lens. My journey began with a simple point-and-shoot camera and has evolved into a full-time dedication to the craft of visual storytelling.

I believe every photograph should evoke emotion, tell a story, and preserve memories that would otherwise fade. Whether it's the golden hour light on a mountain peak or the quiet intimacy of a wedding ceremony, I approach each project with the same level of care and creativity.
